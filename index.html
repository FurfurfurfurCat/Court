One-time Safari setup for Tennis Venues
This is only needed for the six Tennis Venues locations. Court Capture reads the times displayed after you sign in; it does not copy your password or cookies.
  1. Install the free Userscripts Safari extension ↗.
  2. On iPhone, enable it in Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions.
  3. Open Court Capture for Safari, open the Userscripts extension menu, and approve the installation.
  4. Allow Userscripts on tennisvenues.com.au, then sign in there once.
  5. Return here and press Check Availability. The official pages will return automatically.
Keep Safari open while Court Search moves through the selected venue and date pages.
